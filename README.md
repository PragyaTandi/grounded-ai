# 🌱 Grounded

**Touch grass. Explore your world. Earn rewards.**

Grounded is an open-AI-powered exploration app designed to encourage people to step away from their screens and discover the real world.

## 💡 The Idea

Users share their interests, budget, available time, and whether they're exploring alone or with friends. An AI adventure planner helps turn those preferences into real-world activities.

The long-term vision is to reward people for exploring new places, revisiting local favourites, and sharing experiences with friends.

## ✨ Planned Features

* 🤖 AI-generated personalised adventures.
* 📍 Discovery of interesting places and activities.
* 👫 Shared adventures with friends.
* 🎁 Points and rewards for real-world exploration.
* 🔓 Open-weight AI with the potential for local inference.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology

* Frontend: React + TypeScript
* Backend: Python + FastAPI
* AI: Open-weight language model
* Local inference: Ollama

## 🌍 Why Open AI?

Open-weight models provide opportunities to experiment with different models, customise AI behaviour, and run inference locally on compatible hardware.

Grounded explores how open AI can help people discover the world while reducing dependence on closed AI services.

## 🚀 Project Status

Hackathon prototype in development. Features will be updated as they are implemented and tested.
